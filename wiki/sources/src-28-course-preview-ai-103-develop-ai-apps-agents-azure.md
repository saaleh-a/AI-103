## TL;DR

Rob Foulkrod presents AI-103 as a course for moving from clever prototypes to business-dependent AI apps and agents that reason over data, use tools, collaborate with other agents, and bring voice, vision, and documents into solutions (SRC-28 L4–24). The preview says the intermediate course teaches building, deploying, and managing AI apps and intelligent agents with Microsoft Foundry (SRC-28 L39–43). It emphasizes practical labs, Python/API/SDK readiness, Microsoft Foundry, agents, multimodal AI development, certification preparation, and instructor-led or self-paced delivery (SRC-28 L69–151).

## Key claims

- The preview frames the course around business-ready AI solutions that reason over data, take action with tools, work with other agents, and incorporate voice, vision, and documents (SRC-28 L4–24).
- Rob Foulkrod identifies himself as a Microsoft Trainer specializing in developer, DevOps, and AI courses (SRC-28 L25–28).
- The course is AI-103: Develop AI Apps and Agents on Azure (SRC-28 L29–34).
- The course teaches building AI agents that do useful work (SRC-28 L35–38).
- The preview says the intermediate course teaches how to build, deploy, and manage AI apps and intelligent agents using Microsoft Foundry (SRC-28 L39–43).
- Covered topics include generative AI chat apps, multi-agent workflows, voice and vision experiences, and extracting insights from documents and other content (SRC-28 L44–52).
- Learners should learn to ground models in their own data, give agents tools, and orchestrate multiple collaborating agents (SRC-28 L53–59).
- Practical experience includes publishing agents where users work, adding speech and vision to apps, and applying responsible AI guardrails to move from prototype to production (SRC-28 L60–68).
- The course includes interactive exercises and guided practical labs across the learning paths in Microsoft Foundry (SRC-28 L69–75).
- The target learner has working knowledge of Python, experience calling REST APIs and SDKs, and foundational knowledge of Azure services and generative AI concepts (SRC-28 L76–82).
- The preview says the course helps focus on the Microsoft Certified Azure AI Apps and Agents Developer Associate, AI-103, but also says work experience and practice are required for true preparedness (SRC-28 L83–95).
- The training is available as self-paced material or instructor-led sessions with Microsoft trainers (SRC-28 L130–134).

## How it works

The preview uses a problem-to-course pitch: business requests require dependable AI solutions, so the course teaches Foundry-based apps, agents, tools, grounding, orchestration, multimodal capabilities, responsible AI, and deployment/publishing practice (SRC-28 L4–68). It positions hands-on labs as the reinforcement mechanism and names the prerequisite skill base of Python, REST APIs, SDKs, Azure, and generative AI fundamentals (SRC-28 L69–82).
