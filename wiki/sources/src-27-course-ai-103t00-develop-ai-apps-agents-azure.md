## TL;DR

This official course page describes AI-103T00-A as an intermediate, four-day Azure and Microsoft Foundry course for AI engineers and developers (SRC-27 L203–210). It says the course targets software developers building AI-infused applications with Microsoft Foundry, including generative AI apps, agents, knowledge connections, tools, multimodal capabilities, and complex content understanding (SRC-27 L214–217). The syllabus is summarized as four learning paths: generative AI apps, AI agents, natural language solutions, and visual data extraction (SRC-27 L221–240).
